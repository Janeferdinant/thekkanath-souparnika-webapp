Chapter 3: Customer Enquiries & Lead Pipeline (/admin/enquiries)

1-Click WhatsApp Connection: Tap the WhatsApp icon on any inquiry card to launch a pre-formatted greeting confirming date availability. Once the family agrees, click "Convert to Booking" to transfer all contact details directly into the booking engine!

Chapter 4: The Booking Engine & Conflict Prevention Safeguard

Zero Double-Bookings Safeguard The built-in conflict engine evaluates hall, date, and time overlaps before confirming. If another staff member took the same date, the system immediately rejects the overlap and alerts you with the existing booking reference.
