2. The Critical "Nodal Point" Rule (Avoiding Stitch Errors)

The single most important principle in 360 photography is keeping the camera lens at a fixed point in space:

❌ WRONG: Swinging Arms Around Body

Result: Parallax tearing. Auditorium stage pillars, handrails, and dining tables appear sliced and misaligned.

✅ CORRECT: Body Rotating Around Phone

Result: Seamless alignment. The background stitches flawlessly across all banquet tables, lights, and floor lines.

3. Inside the Viewfinder: How the Target Dot System Works

Hold phone in portrait (vertical). Turn slowly until the blue target dot enters the center ring. The shutter fires automatically.

4. The 5 Vertical Elevation Rings (Complete 360° × 180° Sphere)

To capture all walls, ceiling, and floor, complete 5 circular passes at these vertical angles:

0°
Pass 1: Horizon
Eye level. Full 360° loop capturing all 4 walls.
+45°
Pass 2: Upper
Tilt up 45°. Captures stage lights and AC vents.
+90°
Pass 3: Zenith
Look straight up to complete top sphere.
-45°
Pass 4: Lower
Tilt down 45°. Captures seating & tables.
-90°
Pass 5: Nadir
Point straight down at floor. Step back slightly.
